### Core Advisory Architecture & Safety Rule

The most critical design constraint of this subsystem is its strict safety invariant:
- **AI Universe is strictly advisory**: It NEVER calls exchange APIs, NEVER signs transactions or private keys, and NEVER executes trades directly.
- Autonomous trading bots send performance telemetry (equity, win rate, profit factor, drawdown, consecutive loss streaks, per-strategy metrics, regime data, and recent closed trades) on a schedule or upon drawdown events.
- AI Universe runs a structured multi-agent consultation and returns bounded parameter recommendations. The trading bot independently validates these recommendations against its own deterministic hard bounds before applying them.
