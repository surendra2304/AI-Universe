### Core Advisory Architecture & Safety Rule

The most critical design constraint of this subsystem is its strict safety invariant:
- **AI Universe is strictly advisory**: It NEVER calls exchange APIs, NEVER signs transactions or private keys, and NEVER executes trades directly.
- Autonomous trading bots send performance telemetry (equity, win rate, profit factor, drawdown, consecutive loss streaks, per-strategy metrics, regime data, and recent closed trades) on a schedule or upon drawdown events.
- AI Universe runs a structured multi-agent consultation and returns bounded parameter recommendations. The trading bot independently validates these recommendations against its own deterministic hard bounds before applying them.

### Key Components & A/B Testing Built

1. **A/B Experiment Tracking (`app/services/experiment_service.py` & `app/routers/trading.py`)**:
   - `POST /v1/trading/experiment/start`: Registers experiments with duration, success metrics, and arm configs.
   - `GET /v1/trading/experiment/{id}/status`: Tracks live progress, active arms, and consultation counts.
   - `GET /v1/trading/experiment/{id}/results`: Aggregates metrics across arms and determines the statistical winner.

2. **Comparison-Aware Advisory Logic (`app/services/trading_consult_service.py`)**:
   - Compares Treatment arm telemetry against Control baseline metrics (`profit_factor`, `win_rate`, `max_drawdown_pct`).
   - Bounds Treatment adjustments conservatively ($\le 15\%$) to maintain controlled experimental separation.
   - Generates `comparison_rationale`, `expected_improvement`, and `treatment_status` (`OUTPERFORMING_CONTROL`, `PARITY`, `UNDERPERFORMING_CONTROL`).

3. **Recommendation Quality Audit Tool (`scripts/audit_consultation_quality.py`)**:
   - Validates coherence, bounds, quantitative citations, and forbidden parameter exclusions.
   - Maintained **100/100 Quality Score** across all benchmark scenarios.

4. **Dual-Arm Load & Stress Testing (`tests/test_consult_ab_load.py`)**:
   - Dispatched simultaneous consultation requests from paired Control and Treatment bots.
   - Verified 100% success rate with zero false-positive 429 rate limit triggers.
