# 🗓️ AI Universe — 24 August 2026

**Day 1 &nbsp;·&nbsp; Foundation & 7-Provider Gateway**

---

Today was all about laying the foundation. I started the AI Universe project from scratch — the idea being a local-first intelligence system where a team of specialized AI agents collaborate to solve complex problems, rather than relying on a single model.

I connected 7 permanently free cloud providers: Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral AI, OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM, Cohere, and HuggingFace. Each one has its own adapter with unified error handling and automatic retry logic. I also removed Cloudflare Workers AI early on because it was too flaky to trust.

I registered 10 specialist agent personas, each assigned to a specific domain and mapped to the best available model for that role. Researcher talks to Gemini. Architect and Critic go through Groq. Coder uses HuggingFace. Debugger and Strategist use NVIDIA. Security Analyst and Fact Checker use Mistral. Data Analyst routes through OpenRouter. And the Synthesizer, who merges everything into the final answer, runs on Cohere.

I built the SQLite persistence layer to track every task, run, message, and evaluation locally. Everything the agents do gets recorded — which model was used, how long it took, what score it got.

Finally, I implemented the initial multi-agent debate engine with problem framing, parallel analysis rounds, critique, rebuttal, evidence checking, and consensus synthesis. It was the first working version — more structured and rigid than I wanted, but functional.

By the end of the day, all **65 tests passed** cleanly.

---

**End of Day State**: AI Universe is live locally with 7 cloud providers, 10 specialist agents, SQLite memory, and a working debate engine.
