## Foundational Architecture Implementation
1. **Project Setup & SQLite Schema**:
   - `pyproject.toml` configured with FastAPI, Uvicorn, httpx, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy, aiosqlite, and Rich.
   - `app/memory/sqlite.py` establishes persistent storage (`data/universe.db`) with tables: `agents`, `tasks`, `runs`, `messages`, `memories`, `strategies`, `experiments`, and `evaluations`.
2. **Multi-Provider Gateway**:
   - `app/providers/base.py` defining `BaseLLMProvider` contract.
   - Adapters for Groq, Cerebras (`app/providers/cerebras.py`), Mistral, OpenRouter, Gemini, NVIDIA, Cohere, and HuggingFace with environment-based credential loading.
3. **The 10 Specialist Agents**:
   - `app/agents/registry.py` and `app/agents/roles/__init__.py` registering 10 specialist agents: Researcher, Architect, Coder, Debugger, Security Analyst, Data Analyst, Critic, Fact Checker, Strategist, Synthesizer with distinct system prompts and provider assignments.
4. **The 6-Round Debate Protocol (`app/agents/debate.py`)**:
   - Round 0: Problem framing (Strategist)
   - Round 1: Independent analysis (10 Specialists)
   - Round 2: Cross-review & adversarial critique (Critic / Fact Checker)
   - Round 3: Rebuttal & defense (Primary specialists)
   - Round 4: Evidence & claim check (Fact Checker)
   - Round 5: Consensus synthesis (Synthesizer)
   - Round 6: Confidence / uncertainty evaluation & preserved dissent
5. **FastAPI Interface & Security (`app/main.py`, `app/api/friday_routes.py`)**:
   - `POST /v1/friday/ask` and `POST /v1/friday/debate` secured via `X-FRIDAY-API-Key` header dependency.
   - Strictly conforms to the required response JSON schema: `task_id`, `answer`, `mode_used`, `agents_used`, `models_used`, `confidence`, `unresolved_disagreements`, `key_evidence`, `run_id`.
