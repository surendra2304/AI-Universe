3. **`GET /agents`** (Public REST):

## Response Model Metadata in `/v1/friday/ask` and `/v1/friday/debate`
- Every debate or ask response payload returns:
  - `agents_used`: List of participating agent IDs (e.g. `["architect", "security_analyst", "critic", "synthesizer"]`)
  - `models_used`: List of exact active cloud models evaluated (e.g. `["meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct", "mistral-small-latest", "gemini-3.5-flash", "command-r7b-12-2024"]`)
  - `mode_used`: `"consensus"` (when early agreement was reached in Round 1) or `"debate"` (when 6-round adversarial deliberation occurred).
