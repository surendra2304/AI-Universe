# 🗓️ 2026-08-25 — Day 2

**Today I made AI Universe genuinely fast.**

---

The 6-round debate loop I built yesterday was correct, but slow. I knew that going in — it was always meant to be a prototype. Today I tore it out and replaced it with something much better.

The new engine works like this: when a query comes in, the relevant specialist agents start thinking **at the same time** using `asyncio.gather`. They don't wait for each other. They don't know what the others are writing. They just go. Then the Synthesizer reviews all the independent perspectives and checks whether they're pointing in the same direction. If they are — and most of the time they will be — it merges them into a single, confident final answer on the spot. The whole thing completes in seconds.

Full debates still happen. But only when they need to. If the Synthesizer spots a genuine contradiction — something that starts with `CONFLICT_DETECTED:` — it triggers a targeted one-round rebuttal with the Adversarial Critic to actually resolve the disagreement. This keeps the quality bar high without making every query feel like a trial.

I call this the "Collaborate First, Debate on Conflict" model. It sounds like a philosophy but it's really just better engineering.

---

After the engine rewrite, I built out the FRIDAY integration layer. FRIDAY needs to be able to query AI Universe directly, and for that I needed a set of clean, authenticated REST endpoints. I added these under `/v1/friday/`:

- `GET /v1/friday/status` → lists active agents, configured providers, and available model names.
- `GET /v1/friday/agents` → full catalog of all 10 agents with roles and model assignments.
- `GET /v1/friday/info` → platform version and overall system health.
- `POST /v1/friday/ask` and `POST /v1/friday/debate` → submit tasks with full audit trail logging.

Everything goes through `X-FRIDAY-API-Key` header auth. No key, no access.
