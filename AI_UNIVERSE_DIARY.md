## What is AI Universe?

AI Universe is my local-first, multi-agent intelligence platform. Instead of relying on a single AI model, I built a panel of 10 specialized AI agents — Architect, Coder, Security Analyst, Researcher, Debugger, Data Analyst, Critic, Fact Checker, Strategist, and Synthesizer — that collaborate in real-time to answer complex technical questions.

The agents think in parallel, and if they agree, their responses are merged instantly. If there's a genuine technical conflict or a security concern, a targeted debate kicks in to resolve it. The whole system runs on 7 permanently free cloud AI providers: Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM, Cohere, and HuggingFace. If any provider is slow or fails, it automatically falls back to the next one.

FRIDAY, my AI assistant, can query AI Universe securely via REST API endpoints to get multi-agent validated answers.
